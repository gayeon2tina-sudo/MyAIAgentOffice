Privacy at My HQ

Updated October 12, 2026. My HQ is a personal productivity app created by GaYeon Ji.

Google sign-in

If you sign in, Google provides your account identifier, name, email address and profile picture to Supabase, our authentication and database provider. This identifies your private app account. Signing in does not grant My HQ access to your Gmail, Google Drive or Google Calendar.

Your office data

Your company settings, departments, tasks, goals, reviews, idea bank and tracker settings are stored in your browser. When signed in, this app data is also saved in Supabase under your account, with access rules separating users. The project administrator can administer the database. Up to 50 previous saved versions are retained for recovery; these are not independent backups.

Browser-only mode does not sync your office to Supabase. Clearing browser data can erase that local office. On first sign-in, you choose whether to copy your browser office to the account. Signing out does not delete your cloud account.

Optional trackers and other services

Connecting a Google Sheets tracker lets the app read the sheet you provide; it does not write changes back. Sheets must be accessible through the tracker connection. Do not publish a sheet containing information you do not want accessible to others. LinkedIn messages are not automatically accessed by this app.

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Google and Supabase process authentication requests, and hosting providers may retain operational logs under their own policies. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. The app does not include advertising trackers or sell your office data.

Your choices

You can use browser-only mode, download account backups from Account & backup, and sign out. For account data deletion or privacy questions, contact GaYeon Ji through the channel where she shared the app with you. Deletion is currently handled by the project administrator, not an in-app button. Do not post private account information in public issues. Keep downloaded backups somewhere private.
